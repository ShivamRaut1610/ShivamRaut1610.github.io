I started in code. Then I got obsessed with a different question — not how to build software, but why most software quietly fails to reach people.

In 2026, my team competed in the PFRDA National Hackathon. The problem: 92% of working Indians under 35 had never opened a pension account — not because they couldn't afford it, but because the product was incomprehensible. The government had been trying for years with information portals and PDF guides.

We didn't fix the pension system. We fixed the story around it. We built PensionQuest — a gamified quest engine with vernacular voice AI (Hindi, Marathi, English) that translated 30-year financial projections into something a 22-year-old in Pune could actually care about. We ranked All India #2.

I am a creative, thoughtful designer who understands design end-to-end: from user psychology and cognitive clarity down to pixel-level CSS. Design without distribution remains unseen; technology without thoughtful design feels inaccessible. I bring the two together so software feels natural, respectful, and genuinely useful.

Whether architecting a rural ecosystem (Kalpataru Labs), a multilingual voice health companion (Pulses.life), or financial awareness systems (PensionQuest & Arthvo), my standard is grounded in empathy: if it doesn't work for a real person with limited time and real-world constraints, it hasn't been built well enough.

01 🏆 AIR #2 National Winner

PensionQuest

The real problem wasn't technology. 92% of Indian youth under 35 had no pension account — because the existing tools spoke like government forms. We spoke like a game.

Built a gamified NPS awareness engine with RPG quest paths, real-time compounding visualizers, and a vernacular voice AI coach in Hindi, Marathi, and English. The deterministic math core runs zero LLM computation — AI only narrates verified numbers.

India's first gamified financial awareness universe — built on Next.js 16 App Router and React 19. Features an interactive delay-penalty wealth simulator, 6-language localization, quest progression system, and a custom design system in Tailwind v4.
