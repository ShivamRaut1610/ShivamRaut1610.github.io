ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Architectural Patterns in Practice

How we solve the hallucination and governance challenges of production Generative AI.

Zero-Hallucination Financial Architecture

In high-stakes domains (PFRDA PensionQuest and SIH Nishchay), LLMs are strictly forbidden from performing mathematical calculations, eligibility checks, or interest computations.

  • Deterministic Core: Python/Pydantic rules engine computes all yields, taxes, and compound projections with mathematical precision.
  • CI Linter Boundary: Strict AST-level lint checks reject any PR importing LLM or OpenAI/Claude modules inside core/deterministic/.
  • AI Narration Layer: Claude & Sarvam AI ingest pre-calculated facts and convert them into natural, empathetic vernacular voice notes.

Multi-Agent Autonomous Compliance (ITCWMS)

Rather than relying on a single monolithic prompt, ITCWMS partitions software governance into distinct, role-specialized AI agents connected to an event bus.

  • RequirementAgent: Validates user story completeness, security requirements, and acceptance criteria.
  • DevelopmentAgent: Audits commits, diffs, and lint scores before pull requests can merge.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Threshold: Decisions below an 85% confidence threshold halt automation and alert senior engineering leads via n8n.
